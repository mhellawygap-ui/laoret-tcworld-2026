Why tekom fits Laoret

tcworld & tekom is one of the world’s largest events for technical communication, held in Germany, Laoret’s priority buyer market. The people who own manuals, terminology, AI translation and localization vendors are all in one place for three days. That makes it the most efficient way to meet the buyers of Laoret’s three focus areas: regulated content (premium), MTPE + quality governance (core) and SaaS & technical content (growth).
