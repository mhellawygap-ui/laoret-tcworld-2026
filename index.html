1. The ROI principle

Laoret should arrive in Stuttgart with a calendar already containing qualified meetings. Walk-in booth traffic is upside, not the business case.

Before Stuttgart
Create demand

Research accounts, identify buying signals, enrich 2–3 POCs per priority account and book meetings.

At the event
Convert interest

Use the 4 sqm booth as a meeting point, scan leads, qualify quickly and capture next steps.

After the event
Create pipeline

Move meetings into samples, audits, proposals, MLV onboarding and partner opportunities.

Management should evaluate tcworld on meetings → SQLs → proposals → pipeline → won revenue, not badge scans or visitor volume.

3. Laoret ICP for this campaign

The campaign follows the internal Laoret market architecture: Germany/DACH and Europe are priority buyer markets for regulated, technical and high-stakes multilingual content, with strong emphasis on life sciences, SaaS/tech, technical documentation and AI-governed localization.

Premium engine

Life Sciences & Regulated

IFUs, clinical/medical content, medical devices, compliance-sensitive documentation and terminology.

Core engine

MTPE + LQA Governance

Human-controlled AI translation, terminology, measurable quality and scalable multilingual workflows.

Expansion engine

SaaS + Technical Content

Software/UI, developer/technical documentation, product knowledge, eLearning and multimedia.

4. What counts as a buying signal?

Localization / language ops

  • MT/LLM/AI translation project
  • Terminology or LQA initiative
  • TMS/CCMS migration
  • Localization hiring

Product / market expansion

  • New product markets
  • New language/UI releases
  • Global support expansion
  • International training/eLearning

Technical / regulated content

  • IFUs / medical documentation
  • Digital manuals
  • Knowledge graph / AI-ready content
  • Compliance and document-control initiatives

7. Outreach motions by segment

Direct customers

Signal → relevance → meeting

Reference the exact session/project/signal. Connect it to one Laoret capability. Offer a useful benchmark or audit. Ask for 15 minutes with two concrete slots.

MLV / LSP

Capacity → specialist fit → vendor conversation

Lead with regulated/technical delivery, European-language strength, MTPE/LQA and overflow capacity. Do not pitch generic market-entry consulting.

Technology partner

Ecosystem → joint value

Discuss referral, co-delivery and the human-governed service layer around TMS/MT/CCMS platforms.

Germany / DACH

Buying signal → Stuttgart trigger

“Our team will be in Stuttgart Nov 10–12…” gives a natural reason to request a meeting at the event or nearby office.

9. Booth operating model

Meeting base

Pre-book the booth around Tier-1 meetings; keep 15–20 minute slots and buffer time.

Fast qualification

Capture account, role, use case, languages/markets, timeline, current setup and next step.

Same-day follow-up

Every serious conversation gets a next action before the day ends: sample, audit, intro, proposal or follow-up call.
